# Death Tracker Pro

Know exactly where you die.

- Records **every death by percentage**, total attempts, completions and best progress, per level.
- **Death map**: a bar chart of where you die most.
- **Progress graph**: how far each attempt got, with your running best.
- **Session stats** and an **improvement trend** (last 10 attempts vs the 10 before).
- Live **HUD** while you play, fully customizable (corner, size, opacity, fields, accent color).
- Stats are saved between sessions automatically.

Open the stats menu from the **Stats** button on the pause screen or from a level's info page.
